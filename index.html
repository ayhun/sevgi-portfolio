About Me

I am a researcher specializing in artificial intelligence and medical informatics. Currently, I am honored to be an Alexander von Humboldt Fellow, conducting research at the Technical University of Munich (TUM) within the Institute for AI and Informatics in Medicine. My work focuses on bridging the gap between advanced computational methods and clinical applications, leveraging machine learning to solve complex problems in healthcare and medical imaging.
